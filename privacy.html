Privacy

This is a demonstration website. Its enquiry form is not connected to KRM or any third-party service. Information typed into the form remains on the page, is never transmitted or stored, and is cleared after the demonstration response.

Direct contact

If you choose the email or phone links, your own email or phone application will handle the communication. Any information you then send is provided directly to KRM Consulting and is outside this demonstration form.

Analytics and storage

This preview does not include analytics, advertising pixels, a newsletter, a customer database or browser storage. It does not intentionally set tracking cookies. Like most hosted websites, the hosting provider may process limited technical request data to deliver and secure the site.

External content

Google Fonts are loaded to display the site’s typography. Demonstration videos use YouTube’s privacy-enhanced domain and are loaded only after you choose to play one; YouTube may then process technical data under its own privacy terms. Links to YouTube, LinkedIn and AusMumpreneur open external services with their own policies.
